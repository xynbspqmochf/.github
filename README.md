## 🛡️ Real talk — what win rates can't tell you

- 📉 **Past performance ≠ future results.** A 70% wallet still loses 3 of 10 — sometimes right after you start following
- 🎣 **Some wallets farm their followers.** Buy quiet → wait for copy-flow → dump. The multi-wallet signal and exit alerts reduce this — they don't delete it
- 🧠 **Survivorship bias is real.** The screener shows today's winners; yesterday's winners who blew up aren't on the list
- ⚠️ **This is information, not advice.** The tracker finds the sharpest wallets — the risk is still yours
